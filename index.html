Comedy

I perform stand-up comedy under the name Leelee Wehrli. Leelee comes from my paternal grandmother Lena’s side of the family (there have always been plenty of Lee nicknames), and Wehrli is from my maternal line as far back as it goes. I use a stage name for comedy so my personal and professional lives stay separate. I previously performed as Dinah St. John, but wanted something more rooted in actual family history.
